# Ideas for later

Not planned for a specific release. Anything here should stay optional and local, and the tool should stay quiet by default.

## Light game touches

- A counter in the help panel: "214 distractions dissolved on 18 sites". Stored locally.
- A clean-page score after pressing Done: "You freed 31% of this page".
- More unlockable effects at milestones. Black hole already unlocks by finishing the practice page. A second route, such as a number of dissolves, would help people who skip the practice.
- Small achievements ("Popup Slayer", "Used the big hammer"), with one switch to turn all of it off.

## Practice page

- More levels: keyboard-only, a page with an iframe ad, remembering items after a reload.
- A timer or best-time display for the popup level.

## Picking

- Whole-site scope for remembered items, as an explicit choice. It can hide the wrong thing on other pages, so it should never be the default.
- Per-item undo (Ctrl+Z in pick mode) instead of only Unhide All.
- Letting the picker work inside iframes the extension owns or can reach.

## Ruled out for now

- Leaderboards or sharing through a server. It would break the "nothing leaves your device" promise. A locally generated image of your stats would be the safe version.
- Ad hunt on real sites, which needs the extension to decide what counts as an ad.
- Injecting fake popups into real sites for a game.
